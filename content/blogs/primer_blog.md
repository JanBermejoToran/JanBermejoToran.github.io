# Welcome

This blog is a record of what I build, investigate, and learn along the way.

Here I’ll document projects, labs, experiments, mistakes, and discoveries mainly related to systems, networking, programming, and cybersecurity. Not just the final result, but also the process behind it: what I tried, what failed, why it failed, and how I managed to solve it.

I’m particularly interested in understanding things from the ground up. I don’t want to stop at *“this works”*, but get to *“I understand why it works.”* Whenever I come across something I believe is worth investigating, I’ll try to take it apart, test it, and rebuild my understanding of it until I fully understand how it works.

Some posts will be complete projects; others will be small investigations, configurations, or experiments that may not have any immediate practical use. That’s precisely why I want to keep them: over time, this site should become a record of my technical growth and a reference for the things I’ve discovered. In other words, it gives me a place to keep track of what I’m working on.

If someone finds a solution here to a problem they were facing, great. And if something I write turns out to be incorrect, even better: it means there is something worth investigating again. If you come across anything, feel free to get in touch with me and I’ll see what I can do to fix it.
